About Us

We specialize in delivering precision machined components that meet the highest standards of quality and reliability. Located in Gokul Shirgaon MIDC Kolhapur, Maharashtra, our machine shop is equipped with advanced CNC, VMC, and HMC machines, along with state-of-the-art measuring instruments including a Digital Height Gauge and CMM.

With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we provide machining solutions for industries such as automotive, hydraulics, power transmission, agriculture machinery and custom OEMs.

Quintessence of our business is ‘Just in Time’ delivery!
